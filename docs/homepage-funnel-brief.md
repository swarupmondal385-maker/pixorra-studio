# Pixorra homepage: funnel brief

Who lands here: a local business owner who has never heard of Pixorra. They got a WhatsApp
message, a reel, or a link. They are on a phone, busy, and sceptical. We have about 5 seconds.

## The one job of the page

Turn a stranger into a booked free demo. Every section either builds belief or removes a
reason to say no. Anything that does neither is cut.

## Order of belief (what the visitor must feel, in order)

1. **"This is for me."** Hero hook names them: local businesses who can't afford to be ignored.
2. **"These people are real."** Big video, real face of the studio, made in Kolkata.
3. **"They get my problem."** Story: getting found is hard, too many vendors.
4. **"They solve it in one go."** Solution cards you can open to see exactly what you get.
5. **"They've done it before."** Live demos of real sites by niche.
6. **"It's simple and safe."** 10-day flow, demo before payment, 20% / 80%.
7. **"I know the price."** Two clear packages: Development, Social Content.
8. **"Others trust them."** Reviews.
9. **"Do it now."** Founder note, then a closer that makes booking obvious.

## Hook rules

- Short sentences. One idea per line. Talk like a person, not an agency.
- Every claim is something we can show (demo, live site, price, timeline).
- Repeat the safest offer everywhere: **free demo in 48 hours, pay only if you love it.**
- One primary action everywhere: **Book free demo.** WhatsApp is the backup.

## Visual rules

- Apple-calm: big type, lots of space, one accent colour per element, no rainbow text.
- Pixorra soul: ink borders, hard shadows, pastel tints, Pixor used sparingly (max once per screen).
- Motion with a purpose: reveal, scroll-driven tracks, a flow that lights up, letters that react.
- Phone first. Every section must read well at 375px before desktop.
